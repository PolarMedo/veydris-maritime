Our Mission

More Than a Fleet.

Veydris Maritime is a fictional maritime organization and community concept created for players who enjoy ships, exploration, and the world of Dynamic Ship Simulator 3.

Exploration

Discover new routes, visit ports, explore the ocean, and take on adventures across the DSS3 world.

Fleet Identity

Build a recognizable fleet with coordinated colors, vessel names, registry numbers, and a shared maritime identity.

Research & Discovery

Give each vessel a role, from Arctic research and exploration to transport and support.

Community

Meet fellow ship enthusiasts, organize voyages, share screenshots, and enjoy the experience together.
